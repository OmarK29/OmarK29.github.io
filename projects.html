Senior Design Capstone · Sep 2025 – Present · Vanderbilt × Schneider Electric

DER Microgrid Protection & Control

250 kW DER capacity
~100 homes served
24 hr dispatch profiles

Leading the protection and control design for a 250 kW islanded microgrid that could power roughly 100 homes, developed in partnership with Schneider Electric engineers Nurulwahida Jamaludin and Jinlei Xing. The project is simulated end-to-end in MATLAB Simscape Electrical, from DER modeling through load dispatch validation.

  • Designed feeder protection schemes to detect, isolate, and clear faults between distributed energy resources and load — matching industry-grade DER coordination practices
  • Developed SOC-based BESS control logic to maintain voltage and frequency stability during islanded operation, including load-following and ramp-rate limiting
  • Built 24-hour load and solar irradiance profiles to validate system dispatch, resilience under cloud transients, and islanding recovery behavior
  • Implemented Git-based version control and standardized team workflows across a multi-person engineering team
  • Partnered directly with Schneider Electric engineers to validate protection architecture against commercial DER integration standards

Rapid Prototyping · Fall 2024 · Lead Electrical & Software Engineer

Automated Syringe Pump

Best Compact Build — Rapid Prototyping, Fall 2024
<$80 total build cost
>90% cheaper than commercial
±0.0001 mL precision

Commercial syringe pumps cost $800 or more. We built one for under $80 that matched market-standard precision. The project was awarded Best Compact Build in the Fall 2024 Rapid Prototyping class and covered the full stack: mechanical design, custom electronics, and real-time embedded firmware.

Completed assembly — compact enclosure with integrated motor drive and display

  • Designed custom motor control hardware and led PCB prototyping and hand soldering
  • Implemented real-time embedded firmware in C++ using Arduino and the AccelStepper library for precise stepper motor control and acceleration profiling
  • Achieved ±0.0001 mL volumetric precision, matching specifications of commercial units costing 10× more
  • Designed the enclosure and all mechanical components in Fusion 360 and fabricated via 3D printing and laser cutting
  • Delivered a full-stack solution: CAD → electronics design → firmware → assembly and calibration

Vanderbilt IEEE Project Showcase

First ever at Vanderbilt
30+ student project teams

The engineering department had high-quality labs and fabrication resources that most students never touched. I founded the Vanderbilt IEEE Project Showcase to close that gap — creating a structured venue where students could pursue personal engineering projects with access to funding, mentorship, and real hardware.

  • Founded and hosted the first-ever Vanderbilt IEEE Project Showcase, establishing a repeatable framework for future events
  • Created a platform for students to pursue technical projects using university lab resources that had historically been underutilized
  • Provided start-up funding and technical mentorship for 30+ student project teams
  • Coordinated logistics, technical requirements, judging criteria, and outreach across multiple engineering disciplines
  • Shifted IEEE's role from passive programming to active technical enablement — students left with tangible project outcomes and presentation experience
